# V7.0.0 Historical Workbook Analysis

Source workbook scope: 2026.4 through 2026.7, six sheets, 7,501 populated group rows, 62 normalized game-title groups.

## Main measurement

- Canonical title present after Unicode, diacritic, punctuation, and spacing normalization: 7,262 rows.
- Share: 96.8138%.
- Non-direct cases: 239 rows.

## High-value legitimate variations

- Acronyms and configured aliases: OPBR, PS99, ROX, GAG/GAG2 and localized equivalents.
- Singularized final title token: Anime Vanguard, LINE Ranger, Blox Fruit, Seven Knight.
- Connector elision while retaining a multi-token title: Seal M CROSS; Ran Mobile Master Class.
- Localized/agglutinated title: FC모바일코리아.
- Punctuation/spacing equivalence: GAG2, GAG 2, GAG-2.

## Historical noise that must not become training truth

Some earlier retained rows show known false-positive forms, including ordinary words containing a short alias and shorter-game attribution inside a numbered sequel. V7 therefore treats the workbook as pattern evidence, not as an unconditional allowlist.

## Runtime implications

- Prefer exact title/alias/query evidence in the group name.
- Use source-query metadata to recover localized or acronym-only valid groups.
- Keep uncertain missing/truncated cards for page verification.
- Reject IP-root-only, sibling-only, and no-evidence cards before browser navigation.
